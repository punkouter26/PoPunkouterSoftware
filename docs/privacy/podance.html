PoDance

Ragdolls that learned to dance

Application ID com.punkoutersoftware.podance
Last updated 28 August 2026

The short version

PoDance does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no server for the app to talk to.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information — no name, email, phone number or address.
  • It does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs or location.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.
  • It shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases.
  • It has no chat, no user accounts, no leaderboards and no way to share anything with other players.
  • It sends nothing to Punkouter Software or to any third party.

What stays on your device

PoDance may save ordinary game state — progress, settings, results — in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, nobody else can read it, and uninstalling the app deletes it. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf because we never receive it in the first place.

Permissions

The app may request the INTERNET permission. That comes from the machine learning framework the game is built on, not from anything the game does; PoDance makes no network requests while you play it. If a future version genuinely needs the network for a feature, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Children

PoDance is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data it does not knowingly collect anything from children of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play listing as well.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about PoDance generally: punkouter26@gmail.com.
