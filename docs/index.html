Punkouter Software

Games whose players learned to move on their own.

Every one of these is a physics simulation with no animation in it. The characters are neural networks trained by reinforcement learning until they worked out, from nothing but a score, how to run, punch, dance or play. Nothing is keyframed and nothing is scripted, which is why they sometimes fall over.
